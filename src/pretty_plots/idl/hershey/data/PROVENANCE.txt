Hershey vector fonts — bundled font provenance
==============================================

Source
------
Origin:  Dr. Allen V. Hershey, U.S. Naval Weapons Laboratory.
         Wolcott & Hilsenrath, "A Contribution to Computer Typesetting
         Techniques: Tables of Coordinates for Hershey's Repertory of
         Occidental Type Fonts and Graphic Symbols", NBS Special
         Publication 424 (U.S. National Bureau of Standards, 1976).
JHF format: James Hurt's conversion of the original NTIS coordinate
         tables into the line-oriented "JHF" text format, distributed
         on USENET in 1989.
Mirror:  https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts
         (the upstream used by the Debian/Ubuntu `libhersheyfont`
         package; listed in the FSF Free Software Directory)
Fetched: 2026-05-03

License
-------
The Hershey fonts are a U.S. Government work and are in the public
domain. The James Hurt JHF redistribution carries the standard notice:

    "This distribution of the Hershey Fonts may be used by anyone for
     any purpose, commercial or otherwise, providing that:
       1. The following acknowledgement must be distributed with the
          font data:
          - The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V.
            Hershey while working at the U.S. National Bureau of
            Standards.
          - The format of the Font data in this distribution was
            originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc.
       2. The font data in this distribution may be converted into any
          other format EXCEPT the format distributed by the U.S. NTIS
          (which carries its own restrictions), provided that the
          original acknowledgement is included with the converted form."

The package's overall MIT license does not apply to these JHF files;
their license travels with the files (as recorded above).
